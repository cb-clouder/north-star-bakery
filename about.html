About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began as a small family bread stand and grew into a neighborhood gathering place. Our purpose has remained the same: to make dependable, thoughtfully prepared baked goods and to know the people we serve.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We choose simple ingredients and work with local grain growers, dairy producers, and fruit farms whenever the season allows. Long fermentation and small-batch preparation help us build flavor without unnecessary additives. We also plan production carefully to reduce food waste.
