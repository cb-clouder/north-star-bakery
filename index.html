Welcome to North Star Bakery

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery where breads, pastries, and celebration cakes are made by hand each day. We welcome local families, commuters, and event planners looking for fresh baked goods and friendly service.

What We're Known For

Our bakers use patient fermentation, simple ingredients, and locally sourced grains whenever possible. Customers visit us for crusty artisan breads, flaky morning pastries, and custom cakes made for life's important moments.
